# Lights Out

An apartment building is still awake. Click each glowing window to switch it off, and keep every window dark until time runs out. A flickering outline warns that a light is about to come back on.

Level 1 starts with six lights and no relights; level 2 has ten lights and one relight; level 3 has fifteen lights on a taller building and three relights. Relights are scheduled between 30% and 70% of the round, leaving time to react. Higher speeds shorten the round and the warning proportionally. Dark windows ignore clicks.

Windows are 80 × 60 logical pixels, with room between them to make rapid clicking manageable. The outcome is checked on the final tick, not when the last light is first switched off.
