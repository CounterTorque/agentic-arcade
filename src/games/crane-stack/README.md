# Crane Stack

## Concept

A crane swings crates over a waiting tower. Drop three crates so each centre lands over the crate below. A miss topples the tower.

## Controls

Space, Enter, Z, J or click to drop a crate. Crates fall straight down without carrying the swing's momentum.

## Difficulty

| Level | Initial swing period | Crate width | Period multiplier per crate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 2.0 s | 120 px | 0.9 |
| 2 | 1.5 s | 100 px | 0.9 |
| 3 | 1.1 s | 80 px | 0.8 |

Swing, falling and the 300 ms respawn delay all scale with speed. The hook swings 230 px either side of centre. Falling accelerates under gravity; the first drop takes about 0.4 s at speed 1 and subsequent drops are shorter. At level 3 and speed 2.5 the final swing takes about 282 ms, making precision important.
