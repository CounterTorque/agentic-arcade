# Bubble Wrap

Click every bubble on the drifting sheet before one escapes off the right edge or time runs out.

## Difficulty

Levels 1, 2 and 3 have 4, 6 and 8 bubbles, scrolling at 20, 40 and 60 pixels per second respectively. Scroll speed is multiplied by the host's speed, which also shortens the round.

## Tuning

The leading column reaches the edge about 65% of the way through the round. Pop that column first, then work back across the sheet. Seeded jitter varies the layout slightly without overlapping bubbles.
