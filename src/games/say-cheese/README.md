# Say Cheese

Three friends wander through the park. Drag the viewfinder to fit all three completely inside, then click to take the photo. A cut-off friend or a missed shot loses.

The friends move faster and range farther apart at higher levels; level 3 starts them moving in opposite directions. All movement scales with the host's speed. Their movement stays within a shared area small enough for the viewfinder, even at the highest difficulty.

The frame is 320×240 logical pixels and each friend fits inside a 28-pixel radius. A gesture must move more than 6 pixels to count as a drag; a stationary click takes the photo.
