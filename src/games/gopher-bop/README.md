# Gopher Bop

Click the brown gophers as they pop out of their holes. Reach the hit target before time runs out, and don't click the white rabbits.

## Difficulty

Level 1 has six holes, five hits to collect, and gophers visible for 900 ms. Level 2 has nine holes, six hits, 650 ms appearances, and one rabbit. Level 3 has twelve holes, seven hits, 450 ms appearances, and two or three rabbits. Appearance durations and the round timer shrink with speed.

## Tuning

Each seeded round offers three extra gophers beyond the target. Pops are staggered across the round and may overlap in different holes; rabbits cannot overlap another critter in the same hole.
