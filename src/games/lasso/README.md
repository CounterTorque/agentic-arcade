# Lasso

A calf runs across the dusty field. Click where it will be to throw your one lasso; the loop must land over the calf.

The calf runs at 150, 220, or 300 pixels per second at levels 1, 2, and 3. Level 2 adds one timed turn; level 3 adds repeated random turns. The calf turns at the field edges. Motion and throw speed scale with the host speed.

The throw takes 400 ms at speed 1 at every level, and the loop catches a calf within 65 pixels of its center. A missed throw, an unfinished throw, or no throw at timeout loses.
