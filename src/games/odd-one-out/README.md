# Odd One Out

Click the one face that differs from the rest. Picking a matching face loses immediately; running out of time also loses. The odd face is revealed after a loss.

Level 1 has a 3×3 grid with an obvious missing eye, frown or contrasting color. Level 2 has a 5×5 grid with a missing eyebrow, tilted hat or moderate shade difference. Level 3 has a 7×7 grid with a subtle shade or rotation difference. The host shortens the four-second round as speed rises; the gentle bob scales with speed and stops for reduced motion.

The faces and their differences are drawn in the same cells used for hit testing. The smallest cells are 78 logical pixels wide, with gaps that do not count as picks. The odd position, difference and palette are seeded for repeatable rounds.
